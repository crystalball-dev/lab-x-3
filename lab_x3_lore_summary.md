# Lab X-3 (Oasis-3) — Lore Summary & Research Guide

**Lab X-3** (internally designated as **Oasis-3**) is a critical underground testing facility in the *S.T.A.L.K.E.R.* universe, primarily featured in *S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl* and integrated heavily into deep lore expansions. Operated under the umbrella of Project X, the lab serves as a key historical pillar for understanding the Group's foundational experiments before the creation of the Zone.

---

## 1. Core Purpose: Hallucinogenics and "Subtle Matter"
Unlike laboratories like Lab X-18, which heavily prioritized physical and biological mutations, **Lab X-3 focused on hallucinogenics and the "Subtle Matter Theory."**

* **The Immortality Goal:** Scientists used this site to study the human mind's capacity to transcend physical form. The ultimate objective was to transfer human consciousness directly into the Noosphere (the Earth's informational field) to achieve a state of immortality.
* **The Neurovisual Reality:** According to characters tied to its history, "subtle matter" within X-3 was largely an engineered illusion. The lab utilized specialized, localized radiation technology to trigger profound neurovisual hallucinations. These experiments were designed to test methods of manipulating human perception, loyalty, and behavior on a massive scale.

---

## 2. History and The Connection to "Dark"
The history of Lab X-3 is directly intertwined with the tragic story of a stalker named **Dark**, which highlights the laboratory's active operational window under the **Group** and **C-Consciousness**.

* **The Investigation:** Dark was originally dispatched to Lab X-3 by the legendary **Doctor** to investigate the Group's active claims regarding digital immortality and the Noosphere.
* **Brainwashing and Programming:** Dark was captured and exposed to the facility's neurovisual programmers. He effectively became a mind-controlled C-Consciousness agent. 
* **The Permanent Damage:** The localized radiation and neural tampering developed in X-3 left severe psychological scars. Those exposed cannot survive further psychological manipulation without violently reacting against or completely breaking under their programming.

---

## 3. The MDST Network and Abandonment
Historically, Lab X-3 operated as a black site under the **Ministry of Defense and Secret Technologies (MDST)**, an obscure Soviet-era offshoot. 

* **Post-Soviet Continuity:** When official government funding froze following the collapse of the Soviet Union, the network continued working in secret, eventually coming under the total control of C-Consciousness.
* **The Scrubbing (2008):** Like many other X-Labs across the Zone, Lab X-3 was officially abandoned and locked down around 2008. The Group attempted to purge their localized databases, leaving behind a heavily fortified, mutant-infested ruin under the Cordon region.
